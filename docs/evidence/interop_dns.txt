=== DNS interop: dig (ISC BIND) → our DnsServer ===

--- A record ---
$ dig +short @collector A collector.node-monitor.local
10.42.0.1

--- AAAA record (IPv4-mapped IPv6 synthesized from the A binding per RFC 4291 § 2.5.5.2) ---
$ dig +short @collector AAAA collector.node-monitor.local
::ffff:10.42.0.1

--- PTR (reverse lookup via the in-addr.arpa chain) ---
$ dig +short @collector -x 10.42.0.1
collector.node-monitor.local.

--- NXDOMAIN (name does not exist in our zone) ---
$ dig +short @collector A nonexistent.node-monitor.local
(empty — correct: server returned NOERROR with 0 answers / NXDOMAIN)

--- REFUSED (name is outside our authoritative zone) ---
$ dig +short @collector A google.com
(empty — correct: server returned REFUSED for off-zone query)
